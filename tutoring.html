Private Tutoring

Hi, I am Sree. I have been tutoring K–12 math since 2018. I started at Mathnasium and, after leaving in 2022, continued working as a private tutor. I earned a perfect score on the math section of the SAT and I genuinely enjoy helping kids and young adults build confidence in math.

On this page you can see the subjects I tutor, pricing, policies, and you can schedule, reschedule, or cancel sessions directly.

Subjects Offered

Focused on building strong foundations and confidence in math and related subjects.

  • Elementary School Math (Grades K–5)

    Ex: Number sense and place value, addition, subtraction, multiplication, division, fractions, decimals, geometry, measurement, time, money, and word problems.

  • Middle School Math (Grades 6–8)

    Ex: Fractions, decimals, ratios, proportions, percent, pre-algebra, equations, inequalities, and word problems.

  • High School Math (Grades 9-12)

    Ex: Algebra 1, Geometry, Algebra 2, Precalculus, Trigonometry, and Honors level support.

  • Test Prep and Enrichment

    SAT math, ACT math, placement tests, summer prep, and extra practice for students who want to go beyond class material.

Tutoring Policies

Cancellation and Rescheduling

  • Please cancel or reschedule at least 24 hours before the session when possible.
  • Last minute emergencies happen. If something urgent comes up, email or text me as soon as you can.
  • Repeated last minute cancellations will result in canccellation fees.

Session Format

  • Sessions are only through Zoom. I send notes from the session via text or email afterwards.

Materials

  • Please send any worksheets, homework pages, or test review sheets at least a few hours before your session if you want to focus on those.
  • Students should have a notebook/iPad, writing utensil/electronic pen, calculator, and a phone (if possible) ready.
